Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Dommy Studios's Watch & Earn platform ("the Service"). Contact: 1dommy.brain@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you create an account and use the Service, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password ourselves), and an optional display name you can set.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you choose "Continue with Google," we receive your email address from Google to create or match your account.
  • Referral data: your unique referral code, and a record of which accounts signed up using it (so referral rewards can be credited correctly).
  • Points and rewards activity: your points balance and a history of how you earned or spent points (videos completed, quiz results, referral bonuses, redemptions).
  • Video watch activity: which videos you've started or completed, and your playback progress. This exists specifically to verify you watched a video before crediting a reward, and to prevent fraudulent claims.
  • Redemption details: the reward type you request (cash, airtime, or data) and the destination (typically a phone number) you provide to receive it.
  • Standard technical data: basic server logs (such as request timestamps) generated automatically by normal web server operation.

We do not collect payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or similar sensitive identifiers through this Service.

How we use this information

  • To create and maintain your account and let you sign in.
  • To track and credit the points you earn from watching videos and completing quizzes.
  • To verify video-watching activity and prevent abuse of the rewards system.
  • To process your redemption requests and send rewards to the destination you provide.
  • To credit referral bonuses correctly.
  • To communicate with you about your account or a specific request, if needed.

Who we share information with

  • Supabase (our authentication and database provider) stores your account and activity data on our behalf.
  • When you redeem a reward, your destination (phone number) is used to actually send that reward, reviewed and sent manually by an administrator.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
