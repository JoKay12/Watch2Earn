Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

How do I earn points?

Go to Explore Videos, pick a video from today's selection, and watch it in full — you can't skip ahead. As soon as you finish it, the video's points are credited to your balance right away. You'll then be asked 3 quick questions about the video — each one you get right adds a bonus on top.

Why can't I skip ahead in the video?

Skipping is disabled on purpose. Rewards are for actually watching the content, not just claiming credit for it — the seek bar, keyboard shortcuts, and fullscreen are all turned off on the player, and your watch progress is verified on our end, not just trusted from your browser.

Why do I have to answer questions after watching?

You don't have to — your video reward is already credited the moment you finish watching, whether or not you take the quiz. The 3 questions are just a chance at bonus points: each correct answer adds extra to your balance, with no downside for getting one wrong.

How many videos can I watch per day?

New accounts see 5 videos a day to choose from, and can earn from up to 3 of them per day. Once you've referred 5 new users who sign up with your code, that expands to 8 videos to choose from, with up to 5 of them earning a reward per day. If you don't get through your daily watch limit, tomorrow's selection is different videos, not a repeat of today's.

How does the referral program work?

Share your referral code or link (found on your Dashboard) with someone new. Once you've referred 5 people, your daily video pool expands from 5 to 8, and your daily watch limit increases from 3 to 5 — referrals don't add points directly, they unlock more earning opportunity per day instead.

How do I redeem my points?

Go to Rewards & Earnings, choose Cash, Airtime, or Data, and pick from the available point tiers you can currently afford. All three reward types are available starting at 2,000 points. You can submit one redemption request per day, for anywhere between 100 and 500 GHS.

How long does a redemption take?

Redemption requests are reviewed and sent manually by an admin, not automatically. Please allow up to 24 hours before your reward arrives at the phone number or account you provided.

My reward hasn't arrived — what do I do?

First, double check the destination (phone number or account) you entered was correct when you submitted the request. If it's been longer than 24 hours and everything was entered correctly, reach out to Dommy Studios through any of the social links in the footer below.

Can I change my redemption destination after submitting?

Not directly. If you made a mistake, contact Dommy Studios through the social links below as soon as possible, before the request is processed.

What happens if I get a question wrong?

Nothing is lost — your video reward is already secured regardless of your quiz score. Getting a question wrong just means missing out on that one question's bonus; it doesn't affect the reward you already earned for watching, and there's no penalty beyond that.
